Welcome to ICSHAW 2027

On the Occasion of Platinum Jubilee Celebration of IIT Kharagpur

ICSHAW 2027 will be held on 1 and 2 February 2027 at the IIT Kharagpur Research Park, New Town, Rajarhat. The conference brings together researchers and practitioners from psychology, public health, economics, education, management and design to examine what makes lives and communities go well — and how that knowledge turns into policy and practice.

Happiness and well-being are increasingly understood as multidimensional, extending beyond income and physical health to encompass psychological, educational, occupational, socio-economic, environmental, cultural, spiritual, and technological dimensions across the life course. This broader perspective is shaped by emerging challenges such as mental health concerns, educational and workplace stress, new modes of work, climate change, socio-economic inequalities, and the growing influence of digital technologies and Artificial Intelligence. Addressing this complexity requires a holistic and interdisciplinary approach that bridges scientific, social, cultural, environmental, and technological perspectives. Such an approach can help advance inclusive, adaptive, and sustainable policies and practices that promote happiness and well-being for individuals, communities, and society as a whole. In addition to paper presentations, the conference will feature keynote addresses and engaging panel discussions with distinguished experts from academia and industry.

About

ICSHAW 2027 is organised by the Rekhi Centre of Excellence for the Science of Happiness of the IIT Kharagpur, as part of the Institute's Platinum Jubilee celebrations marking seventy-five years since its founding in 1951. The conference brings the science of happiness and well-being together with the disciplines that act on it — psychology, public health, economics, education, management and design. This edition is built around interdisciplinary pathways: not only what the evidence shows, but how it reaches policy, workplaces, classrooms and communities.

Call for papers

Abstracts are invited on all aspects of the science of happiness and well-being, from any discipline. Submissions are peer reviewed and accepted contributions appear in the conference proceedings.

Topics

  • Positive psychology and flourishing
  • Mental health and resilience
  • Happiness economics and policy
  • Workplace and organisational well-being
  • Education and student well-being
  • Mindfulness and contemplative practice
  • Digital technology and well-being
  • Measuring well-being
  • Sustainability and inclusive futures
  • Community and social connection

Submission

Papers must be up to 4 pages plus one page of references, anonymised for double-blind review, and submitted in PDF through the submission system.
